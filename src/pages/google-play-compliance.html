04 / THE QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

Every app is different. Here are a few things teams usually want to know up front.

Do I need to update my target SDK?+

Google Play's target API requirements change over time. If your app is receiving warnings or can't publish an update, an assessment will establish what your app needs now.

Will modernization break the app?+

Platform upgrades can change behavior, which is why I work incrementally and test critical flows. The goal is to reduce risk, not simply bump a version number.

How much will it cost?+

That depends on the codebase, dependencies, and release blockers. A free initial audit gives us a clearer starting point before estimating the work.

Can you help with old Gradle or Java code?+

Yes. Older toolchains and Java modules are common in legacy Android apps. They can be upgraded in stages rather than rewritten all at once.

Can you help with React Native or Flutter Android modules?+

Yes. I can work on the native Android side of React Native and Flutter projects when build tooling or Play compatibility is the blocker.

What about ongoing maintenance?+

I can help teams keep apps current after the initial upgrade, including dependency updates, platform changes, and future Play releases.
