01 / MY STORY

I build things that have to actually work.

For more than eight years, I've taken Android products from an early idea to a shipped, maintained experience. I've worked across consumer apps, B2B platforms, marketplaces, wellness, and hardware-integrated systems — often on the tricky work between a product's ambition and its technical reality.

Today, I lead Android engineering at Devsarch, building an in-store device buyback kiosk and a cloud-connected diagnostics platform. Before that, I spent nearly six years at Xenex Media, leading a team of 10+ engineers and delivering products that combined payments, biometrics, localization, and AI.

I care about clean architecture and good interfaces, but I care just as much about the less glamorous parts: maintainable builds, secure data, reliable releases, and a product that keeps working long after launch.
